Bite or Not

Privacy Policy

Effective October 10, 2026

Bite or Not (“the app”) tells you whether a plant, flower or food is safe for your cat or dog. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and how you can delete it. We collect as little as the app needs to work. We do not sell your data, show ads or track you across other apps and websites.

Your account

When you first open the app, it creates an anonymous account: a random identifier, with no name, email or phone number. The sign-in token is kept in your device’s keychain.

You can optionally sign in with Apple (or Google on Android) to keep purchased checks when you change phones. We receive a stable identifier from Apple or Google. We do not ask for your name or email address.

What you check

  • Typed names. The text you enter, the answer we gave (for example “dangerous for cats”) and the time are stored in a check log linked to your anonymous account. We use it to provide the service, count your free and purchased checks, keep the service reliable and see which plants and foods to add to our database.
  • Photos. Photos are made smaller on your phone and their metadata, including location, is removed before upload. They are sent to our server and to OpenAI for analysis, and are not stored by us.
  • Pet choice. Whether you have a cat, a dog or both is kept on your device and sent with each check, so the answer covers only your pets.

Photos and names that are not in our database are analyzed by OpenAI. OpenAI does not use data sent through its API to train its models and may keep it for a limited time (up to 30 days) to detect abuse, under its API data policies.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Apple (or Google on Android); we never see your payment details. RevenueCat, our purchase service, receives purchase information linked to your anonymous identifier. We store the store transaction ID, the pack you bought, its date and your remaining checks.

Technical data

Like any online service, our hosting provider processes your IP address and request details to deliver responses, prevent abuse and rate-limit requests. We keep aggregate statistics such as the number of checks and response times. We do not use advertising identifiers or third-party analytics.

Service providers

  • Supabase — database, accounts and server functions (United States).
  • OpenAI — analysis of photos and of names not found in our database.
  • RevenueCat — processing of in-app purchases.
  • Apple, Google — optional sign-in and payments.
  • GitHub — hosting of this website.

How long we keep data

We keep your account data until you delete your account. When you do, your account, sign-in links, remaining checks and purchase records are deleted, and your check log is anonymized: the identifier and the text you typed are removed, so the remaining statistics cannot be linked to you.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Account → Delete account. Remaining purchased checks are lost. You can also ask us to delete your data — see Delete your account.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, or to object to its processing. Contact us through the Support page and we will respond.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.

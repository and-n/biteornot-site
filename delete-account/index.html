Bite or Not

Delete your Bite or Not account

In the app

  1. Open Bite or Not.
  2. Tap the account button in the top right corner.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Deletion is immediate. The app then starts over with a new anonymous account.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app, contact us through the Support page and ask to delete your account. If you signed in with Apple or Google, tell us which one so we can find your account.

What is deleted

  • Your account and its sign-in links (Apple or Google).
  • Your remaining free and purchased checks — purchased checks cannot be restored after deletion.
  • Your purchase records with us, and your customer record at RevenueCat, our purchase service.

What is kept

Your check log is anonymized: the account identifier and the text you typed are removed, and only aggregate statistics (such as the number of checks) remain. Apple and Google keep their own records of your purchases under their policies.

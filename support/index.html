Questions

How does the app decide?

Names found in our toxicity reference list are answered directly from it. Photos and other names are analyzed with that reference list as context, and the answer is never rated safer than the list says.

What does “Unknown — keep away” mean?

We could not verify the item, for example because it is not in our list or the photo was unclear. Treat it as unsafe and check with your veterinarian.

How many free checks do I get?

Every new account gets free checks. After that you can buy check packs; purchased checks never expire. Unreadable photos, unrecognized items and items we have no data on do not use up a check.

I changed phones. How do I keep my checks?

Before switching, open Account and sign in with Apple (or Google on Android). On the new phone, sign in with the same account.

A purchase went through but my checks did not arrive.

Checks usually arrive within a few seconds. Close and reopen the app. If they are still missing, contact us with the date of the purchase.
